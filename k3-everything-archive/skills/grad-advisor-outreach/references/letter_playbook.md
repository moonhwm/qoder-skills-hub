## 一、收敛范例 A：正式套磁信（T1）

```
Subject: MSc Inquiry — AI for STEM Defect Detection

Dear Dr. Cha,

In your recent Nature Communications paper on phase-engineered VO₂ cathodes, you noted that
"manual structural identification remains the primary bottleneck in high-throughput STEM
screening." This observation directly connects to my own experience: during my undergraduate
work on SiO₂/ZnO thin films (50–200 nm, RF sputtering), I manually correlated deposition
parameters with electrical properties — reducing sheet resistance from 1.2×10⁴ to 1.5×10²
Ω/sq — a process that automated image analysis could accelerate.

Since graduating in 2023, I have focused on clarifying my research direction and building
foundational skills for graduate study. I am now ready to commit to a focused MSc program
at {TARGET_INSTITUTION}, where I hope to contribute to your group's work on
structure-property mapping in energy materials.

Your work bridging fundamental characterization with energy materials development has
informed my approach to moving from experimental thin-film work toward AI-assisted
microscopy. This is why I would like to formally express my interest in position
{POSITION_ID}, and I would welcome the opportunity for a brief email exchange to explore
potential alignment.

I have attached my CV and thesis. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
{APPLICANT_NAME}
[REPLACE_WITH_EMAIL] | [REPLACE_WITH_PHONE]
```

约 175 词。结构：Hook（引用导师原句）→ 数字锚点（自身论文硬数据）→ gap 宽泛主动表述 →
平台锚定 → 导师兴趣段 → 低压力请求。

**为什么收敛于此**：Hook 引用可查证论文原句；数字锚点与 CV 完全一致；gap 段用
"focused on clarifying...building foundational skills"——用户明确确认"宽泛解释 gap 是
可接受的"，回退了早期版本不可验证的具体声称。

## 二、收敛范例 B：约束确认+套磁混合信（T3）

适用：职位描述含 fatal 暗约束（如"intended for students who will transition to PhD"），
先问清楚再决定是否正式申请。

```
Subject: MSc {POSITION_ID} — Physics Background + Interest in AI for Molecular Design

Dear Prof. {NAME},

I am a 2023 B.Sc. in Applied Physics ({INSTITUTION}). My background is in fabricating
SiO₂/ZnO thin films via RF sputtering, where I achieved a 98.7% reduction in sheet
resistance through buffer layer optimization. I have also used MATLAB for systematic data
modeling. Since graduating, I have focused on clarifying my research direction and building
foundational skills for graduate study.

I am now ready to commit to a focused MSc program at {TARGET_INSTITUTION}. Your integration
of ML and molecular simulation for material design has informed my approach to translating
my experimental background into computational materials science. I see a direct connection
between my experience in systematic parameter-property correlation via MATLAB and your
group's use of ML-driven screening: both involve identifying high-performance candidates
from large parameter spaces.

Given my plan to pursue a PhD at a university in {COUNTRY} after the MSc, I would like to
clarify whether the MSc is designed as a standalone 2-year program, or whether transition
to the PhD track is an integrated expectation. If the MSc can be completed as a standalone
program, I would be eager to submit a full application.

Best regards,
{APPLICANT_NAME}
[REPLACE_WITH_EMAIL] | [REPLACE_WITH_PHONE]
```

约 170 词。核心技巧：把约束问题包装在"我有长期规划"的积极叙事里，而非质问。

## 三、范例 C：中文申请跟进邮件（港校，材料已提交无回音）

```
您好，我是申请人{姓名}，申请编号{ID}，申请的是{项目}。我的材料{日期}已确认完整，
正在等待面试安排。由于我面临一个紧急的时间决策，想确认：
1．我的面试是否还能在本周内安排？
2．如果面试通过，本周内能出录取结果？
3．如果赶不上这一批，是否有延期入学或下一批的可能？
我可以随时到{城市}参加面试，也可以在线面试。盼复，谢谢。
```

纪律：**不提"其他 offer"和"缴费 deadline"**——这些信息在沟通中会成为负面信号。

## 四、🔴/🟡/🟢 段落批注体系（与文书老师协作）

| 标记 | 含义 | 操作权限 |
|------|------|----------|
| 🔴 红框 | 不可删除、不可修改核心语义（Hook、数字锚点=防伪水印） | 仅可润色语法/拼写 |
| 🟡 黄框 | 核心信号不可改（gap 解释、PhD 规划），措辞可替换 | 见替换指南 |
| 🟢 绿框 | 包装纸 | 按协作方风格自由润色 |

原则：🔴段落删除后信件失去独特性沦为模板；所有 [REPLACE_WITH_*] 必须替换为真实信息；
交付协作文档时附"如何渲染 .md 文件"的一句话说明（对方可能不熟悉 Markdown）。

## 五、发送前检查清单

| # | 检查项 | 方法 |
|---|--------|------|
| 1 | 标题关键词完整（差异化关键词，非泛化标题） | 目视 |
| 2 | Hook 引用与原文逐字一致 | 对照论文原文 |
| 3 | 数字锚点与 CV/论文完全一致 | 逐项对照 |
| 4 | gap 段核心信号保留（宽泛主动表述） | 目视 |
| 5 | 约束问题表述准确（如 standalone vs integrated） | 对照职位原文 |
| 6 | 所有占位符已替换 | 运行 `scripts/letter_honesty_check.py` |
| 7 | 附件齐全且 ≤5MB | 发送前确认 |
| 8 | 发送时间=周二/周三上午（收件人时区） | 对照时区 |
| 9 | 跟踪表已登记（发送日+7 天跟进提醒） | 更新跟踪文件 |

## 六、引用查证纪律与应急预案

- 信件中每个引用必须能追到具体论文（标题+年份）；列入"论文出处与查证清单"文件一并交付。
- 已删除的引用（早期版本存在但查无此源的）记录在案，**不可加回**。
- 同一数字在两封信中的表述必须一致；CV 与信件逐项核对。
- 应急预案：Hook 论文查无此源 → 整句删除，改用泛化兴趣表述；自身数字与论文不一致 → 以论文为准改信件；自称用了某软件但论文未用 → 删除该声称。
